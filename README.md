## What this does

You enter your loan details. It shows you every rupee you will pay — month by month — for the next 20 years. Then it shows you how to pay less.

## Who this is for

| Who you are | How HomePlan helps you |
|---|---|
| Salaried professional with a new home loan | Understand what you actually owe, month by month. |
| First-time buyer checking eligibility | See exactly how much loan your income supports. |
| Anyone who got a rate hike from their bank | Simulate the new cost and decide your next move. |

## How it works

1. Open the app and tap the pencil icon to enter your loan details.
2. Add any extra payments you plan to make — bonuses, maturities, annual windfalls.
3. Tap Calculate. Every tab updates instantly with your full loan picture.
4. Use the Eligibility tab before applying to know exactly what loan you qualify for.

## Screens

### Dashboard
The financial cockpit of your loan displaying the core overview. It reveals critical highlights including your outstanding scale, loan health score, smart customized prepayment suggestions, and quick actions to export files.

### Payment Schedule
An interactive monthly timeline breaking down every installment. It separates components into direct principal, calculated interest, tax savings, and lists interactive milestones where you cross 25%, 50%, and 75% principal payoff.

### Compare Strategies
A high-contrast sandbox to compare up to four repayment vectors side-by-side. It highlights details of your baseline loan against prepayment and rate-stressed setups to demonstrate how simple tweaks yield immense long-term savings.

### Year by Year
A structural annual rollover ledger compiling 12 monthly installments into cards. It gives neat, high-density logs tracking your interest totals, amortized principal, and your overall cumulative tax deductions.

### Tools
A utility hub loaded with standalone sandboxes for instant simulations. It supplies simple modules including an EMI builder, sliding prepayment impact tests, quick rate shock checks, and premium binary search deadline optimizations.

### Eligibility Checker
An industry-standard eligibility assessment tool. It parses salary, co-applicant variables, credit-worthiness, and regional banking criteria to return maximum safe limits, detailed co-applicant upgrades, and target ITR requirements.

### About
A clean, informative dashboard presenting product context, design guidelines, and professional direct-connect channels. It acknowledges developers, highlights standard open-source libraries, and maintains high visual standards.

## Getting started

### Requirements

| Metric | Target Specification |
|---|---|
| Android Version | Android 8.0 (API 26) or above |
| App Footprint | Approx 8–12 MB |
| Connectivity | No internet connection needed (100% Offline) |

### Install the app

* **Step 1:** Go to the Releases section of this repository.
* **Step 2:** Download the latest `HomePlan-android.apk` file.
* **Step 3:** On your Android phone, go to Settings &rarr; Install unknown apps &rarr; allow your browser or file manager.
* **Step 4:** Open the downloaded APK and tap Install.
* **Step 5:** Open HomePlan-android. Follow the short tutorial on first launch.

### Build from source (for developers)

* **Step 1:** Clone this repo: `git clone https://github.com/Nishanth20/HomePlan-android.git`
* **Step 2:** Open in Android Studio (Hedgehog 2023.1.1 or later).
* **Step 3:** Let Gradle sync complete.
* **Step 4:** Run on an emulator (API 26+) or a physical Android device.

*Note: No API keys or external services required. The app is fully self-contained.*

## Loan eligibility checker

In India, banks do not just look at your gross salary to decide your home loan amount. They use a critical metric called Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR). PSU banks usually allow a maximum of 50% FOIR, private banks permit up to 55%, and NBFCs may stretch to 60%. On a ₹50,000 monthly salary with no other ongoing liabilities, a PSU bank limits your maximum monthly EMI cap to ₹25,000, which behaves as the ultimate mathematical ceiling for your total loan eligibility.

For self-employed applicants, banks judge eligibility entirely on your filed Income Tax Returns (ITR) rather than gross business turnover or unofficial ledger receipts. If your physical ITR records show a declared annual income of ₹4 lakhs despite actually earning ₹8 lakhs cash-in-hand, the underwriting engine will approve the loan exclusively matching the ₹4 lakhs limit. The LoanEligibility tool dynamically reverses this flow, stating the precise minimum ITR declaration required to successfully qualify for your desired loan value.

Adding an immediate relative as a co-applicant (typically a spouse or parent) pools your overall incomes to elevate the eligible ceiling significantly. Furthermore, if the primary or co-applicant registered is a woman, many Indian financial units offer an interest rate discount of 0.05% (5 basis points). The built-in co-applicant analysis models eligibility both individually and combined, highlighting exact potential savings.

## Tax benefits

| Section | Annual cap | Who gets it | What the app shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Section 24(b) | ₹2,00,000 | Any home loan borrower on self-occupied property | Month-wise eligible interest amount + annual cumulative total |
| Section 80C | ₹1,50,000 | Combined with EPF, PPF, ELSS and other eligible investments | Month-wise eligible principal + annual cumulative total |

*These are estimates. Your actual tax benefit depends on your income tax bracket and other investments. Consult a CA before filing.*
